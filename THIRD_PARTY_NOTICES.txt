Android Assist wrapper
  Uses the Android platform WebView and text-to-speech APIs, without bundled
  AndroidX or native model libraries. The APK includes the text/speech models,
  original formula/resistor models, ONNX Runtime Web, and the complete licence
  texts noted above. Optional Ultralytics weights and training data are absent.
  Gradle 9.1.0 (Apache-2.0) and Android Gradle Plugin 9.0.1 (Apache-2.0) are
  build tools; JUnit 4.13.2 (EPL-1.0) is a host-only test dependency. These tools
  are not packaged in the APK. The committed Gradle wrapper retains its
  generated licence headers. https://gradle.org/ and https://developer.android.com/build

Assist app: resistor band reader (backend/static/models/resistor.onnx, trained here)
  Trained on drawings (backend/resistor/render.py) plus re-photographed real
  photos from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded by tools/fetch_resistor_photos.py:
    "<value> 5% axial resistor.jpg" series, CC BY-SA 2.0
    "Metal film resistor.jpg", CC BY-SA 3.0 (testing only)
  Those photos are not included in this repository; the script writes an
  ATTRIBUTION.txt with each author next to the downloaded copies.
  Backgrounds for training pictures are blurred crops of COCO val2017 images
  (https://cocodataset.org, individual Flickr licences); not included here.
  tests/fixtures/resistors/ holds crops of two Commons photos that may be
  redistributed freely: "Electronic-Axial-Lead-Resistors-Array.jpg" (public
  domain) and "Resistor metal film 0.5W 1% 82R.jpg" (CC0).

Assist app: safety and parts lists (backend/static/data/safety.json, parts.json)
  Written for this project from general, widely published safety guidance and
  manufacturer datasheets; each safety rule names the kind of source it rests
  on. They are short curated lists, not authoritative references: have a
  qualified person review them before classroom use.

Formula recognizer training inputs
  EMNIST: https://www.nist.gov/itl/products-and-services/emnist-dataset
  Google Fonts: fonts carry their own licences; retain those when sharing fonts.
  Training inputs are not included in this repository. A release of the trained
  weights should retain their training provenance and review the input licences.

Thermochemistry references
  NIST Chemistry WebBook / NIST-JANAF: source links, phase assumptions and the
  checked subset are recorded in backend/chemistry/data/thermochemistry.json.
  Other heat estimates are explicitly unverified.
